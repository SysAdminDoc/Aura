# Project Research and Feature Plan

**Project:** Aura (`com.freevibe`)
**Cycle:** 13
**Date:** 2026-06-04
**Repo state:** `main`, `4072148`, `versionCode 112`

## Executive Summary

Cycle 13 focused on media licensing, attribution, and takedown operations. Cycle 3 already established the broad provider-compliance matrix. This pass narrowed the work to whether provenance survives through user-visible flows and whether Aura can respond to rights-holder, provider, or community takedown events.

Aura has useful provenance primitives: `Wallpaper` has `sourcePageUrl` and `uploaderName`; `Sound` adds `license`; favorites and wallpaper cache migrations preserve several source fields; detail screens show source/uploader/license for many items; and the licenses screen lists libraries plus some content sources. The remaining gap is operational. A badge or static source list does not prove that every apply/share/download/edit path respects license capabilities, that community uploaders granted rights, or that removed third-party content stops appearing after a source deletion.

Top findings:

- P0: Google Play's IP policy explicitly disallows apps that infringe copyright or encourage downloading copyrighted content without authorization. Aura's listing promotes "YouTube-first ringtone, notification, and alarm discovery" plus video wallpapers, so Play-facing builds need extra care not to encourage unauthorized audio/video downloads.
- P0: `LicensesScreen.kt` lists only a subset of content sources and uses broad labels like "Provider-defined" and "Various CC". It does not show per-item licenses, user-upload rights, Aura Originals source URLs, takedown instructions, or action-level capability limits.
- P1: Community sound/wallpaper uploads store `uploaderId`/`uploaderLabel` but not an explicit license, rights attestation, source URL, model/property release state, or takedown contact. Sound uploads appear in the UI with `license = "User Upload"`.
- P1: The source metadata model is asymmetrical: `Sound` has `license`, but `Wallpaper` does not. Video wallpaper items have source/uploader fields but no source page/license/takedown fields.
- P1: `docs/aura-originals-curation.md` correctly requires CC0, source URL, and sha256 for bundled sounds, but the manifest is still empty and there is no automated evidence gate yet.
- P2: Third-party source deletion is not yet modeled as a state. Cached/favorited/downloaded Reddit, Pexels, Pixabay, YouTube, or other media can outlive source removal unless the app marks it unavailable, deletes mirrors, or limits local-only use with clear copy.

## Current State

- `ContentSource` includes many providers: Wallhaven, Bing, Wikimedia, Reddit, NASA, Freesound, Jamendo, Audius, ccMixter, local, YouTube, Pexels, Pixabay, Klipy, SoundCloud, community, bundled, and AI-generated.
- `Wallpaper` carries `sourcePageUrl` and `uploaderName`, but no normalized license or action-capability field.
- `Sound` carries `license`, `uploaderName`, and `sourcePageUrl`.
- `FavoriteEntity` stores `uploaderName`, `sourcePageUrl`, and other metadata, but not `license`.
- `WallpaperCacheEntity` stores `uploaderName`, `sourcePageUrl`, views, favorites, tags, and colors.
- `Mappers.kt` maps source page/uploader metadata for Wallhaven, Bing, Pixabay, favorites, cached content, and Reddit.
- `WallpaperRepository` maps Pexels `photo.url` and photographer into `Wallpaper`; video wallpaper items include source/uploader but not full provenance metadata in the same shape as `Wallpaper`.
- `FreesoundV2Repository`, `FreesoundRepository`, `CcMixterRepository`, `AudiusRepository`, and `SoundCloudRepository` normalize some sound licenses and source page links.
- `UploadRepository.getCommunityUploads()` maps community sounds with `license = "User Upload"` and blank `sourcePageUrl`; upload metadata does not ask for or store a selected license/rights declaration.
- `WallpaperUploadRepository.uploadWallpaper()` stores name/category/tags/colors/uploader metadata but no license/rights declaration.
- `SoundDetailScreen.kt` shows uploader and license badges when present and shares `sourcePageUrl` or the direct download URL.
- `WallpaperDetailScreen.kt` shows source/uploader context and opens/shares `sourcePageUrl` or the direct image URL.
- `LicensesScreen.kt` lists library licenses and eight content source entries. It does not list ccMixter, Audius, SoundCloud, community upload terms, Aura Originals dynamic entries, AI-generated content, Pexels/Pixabay report links, or per-item attribution.
- `docs/aura-originals-curation.md` requires CC0 Freesound sources, `sourceUrl`, sha256 hashes, and retroactive removal by dropping manifest entries. The tracked `aura_originals_manifest.json` was still empty in earlier cycles.
- `database.rules.json` has a boolean `/moderation/{contentId}=true` path, but no report queue, rights-holder notice workflow, or takedown reason/audit model.

External evidence reviewed:

- Google Play's IP policy disallows apps and developer accounts that infringe intellectual property rights or encourage/induce infringement. It names copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, and proprietary rights, and says apps may need to provide evidence of rights to use copyrighted content.
- The same Play policy flags apps that encourage users to stream and download copyrighted works without authorization and gives soundboards with copyrighted clips as a common violation class.
- Pexels' API guidelines require a prominent Pexels link for API requests, ask developers to credit photographers when possible, prohibit copying Pexels core functionality including wallpaper apps, and document rate limits. Pexels' license page also prohibits redistributing photos/videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms.
- Pixabay's license summary allows free use and modification without attribution but prohibits standalone distribution of unmodified content and warns that recognizable trademarks, people, privacy, and other third-party rights may still require consent.
- Freesound API terms require noncommercial free API use, fair usage, no Freesound replication, and proper credit to Freesound and users according to each sound's license.
- Reddit developer terms require apps to delete or modify Reddit user content and related app content when Reddit content is deleted, protected, suspended, withheld, modified, or removed; apps with user/third-party content need notice-and-takedown processes.
- YouTube API policies prohibit undocumented APIs, downloading/importing/backing up/caching/storing YouTube audiovisual content without prior approval, offline playback, and facilitating copyright infringement. They also require privacy disclosure and user-data deletion processes for API clients.

## Findings

### The provenance model is not complete enough for all content types

Sound provenance is stronger than wallpaper/video provenance because `Sound` has a `license` field. Wallpapers and video wallpapers carry source/uploader/source-page information in some paths but not a normalized license or capability model. This makes it hard to answer whether an item can be applied, edited, exported, shared, bundled, or kept offline.

Planning implication: introduce a shared provenance/capability model that covers source, source page, creator, license, provider terms, content rights status, takedown URL, and allowed actions across wallpapers, videos, sounds, community uploads, bundled content, and AI-generated content.

### Static source licenses are insufficient for user and reviewer evidence

`LicensesScreen.kt` is a useful starting point, but it is static and under-complete. It does not show per-item licenses, dynamic Aura Originals attributions, rights-holder takedown steps, community upload terms, or action limitations. Broad labels such as "Provider-defined" are not enough for users, reviewers, or maintainers.

Planning implication: turn the licenses screen into a provenance hub backed by the provider policy matrix and current content metadata, with a small per-source explainer and a report/takedown link.

### Community uploads need rights attestation before publication

Aura accepts community sounds and wallpapers with name/category/tags, then publishes them with an uploader label and "User Upload" license. That leaves no evidence that the uploader owns or is allowed to share the file, no license for downstream users, and no contact/takedown path.

Planning implication: the upload dialogs should require a rights attestation and license/visibility selection before upload. Metadata should store the selected license, original/third-party declaration, optional source URL, and takedown/report state.

### YouTube needs Play-facing risk containment beyond a provider switch

Cycle 3 already queued a YouTube legal-mode switch. Cycle 13 adds store-facing detail: the Play listing, screenshots, and in-app copy must not encourage unauthorized copyrighted downloads. A Play build likely needs YouTube disabled by default or framed as optional/search-only depending on legal review.

Planning implication: create a distribution profile that can disable YouTube sounds/video-wallpaper paths, hide screenshots/copy that imply unauthorized downloads, and keep non-YouTube sources useful.

### Takedown must handle both community and mirrored third-party content

A community report queue handles Aura-hosted uploads. It does not solve Reddit/Pexels/Pixabay/YouTube/source deletion. If a provider removes content, Aura needs to stop presenting cached catalog entries as live source content and decide what happens to favorites/downloaded local copies.

Planning implication: define a source-deleted/unavailable state and a takedown processing runbook. For community content, remove or hide RTDB/Storage objects. For third-party mirrors, reconcile source status and demote/delete cached metadata according to provider terms.

## Verification Targets

- Fixture content for Pexels, Pixabay, Reddit, YouTube, Freesound, ccMixter, Audius, SoundCloud, community, bundled, AI-generated, and local imports renders source/uploader/license/takedown context correctly.
- Favorites, downloads, share sheets, apply flows, and editors preserve provenance and obey action capabilities.
- Community upload requires rights attestation and stores license/source URL metadata.
- Report/takedown flow can hide community content, route rights-holder reports, and reconcile source-deleted third-party content.
- Play-facing release profile does not promote unauthorized copyrighted downloads and can disable YouTube surfaces without breaking core browsing.
