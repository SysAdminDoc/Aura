# Cycle 146 - Community Guidelines Consent Gate

## Scope

Cycle 146 started after the checked Play App content packet identified UGC
terms/guidelines consent as the next locally actionable blocker. Owner-gated
live callable evidence, hosted deletion URL publication, production Firebase
dry runs, GitHub security settings evidence, signed release dry-run evidence,
and fresh store screenshots remained unavailable locally.

## Policy Finding

Google Play's UGC guidance expects apps with user-generated content to define
and enforce acceptable-use rules, provide in-app report/block flows, and take
moderation action when policy-violating content is reported. Aura already had
reporting, blocking, deletion, takedown, and moderation infrastructure, but
community entry points did not require a versioned guidelines acceptance before
users browsed, uploaded, voted, reported, blocked, followed, or edited creator
profiles.

## Local Evidence

- `docs/distribution/play-app-content.json` recorded the UGC guidelines consent
  action as an open owner action after Cycle 145.
- `PreferencesManager` already held provider enablement flags that could gate
  community work before Firebase calls.
- Community sound, wallpaper, voting, profile, block, and upload repositories
  already depended on `PreferencesManager` or could be given it through Hilt.
- Settings, Sounds, and Wallpapers were the visible community entry points
  that needed consent prompts and disabled-state copy.

## Implementation

- Added a versioned `CommunityGuidelinesPolicy` and persisted accepted version.
- Added a shared `CommunityGuidelinesDialog` and Settings review/reset entry.
- Blocked startup identity warm-up until the current guidelines version is
  accepted.
- Gated community feeds, uploads, votes, reports, blocks, follows, creator
  profiles, and upload finalization behind the accepted guidelines version.
- Prompted users from Sounds and Wallpapers before entering community tabs,
  uploads, recording, or vote actions.
- Added `docs/legal/community-guidelines.md` as the reviewed policy text.
- Updated the Play App content packet so guidelines consent is implemented
  evidence instead of an open owner action.
- Added `tools/community_guidelines_consent_check.py` plus focused tests and
  verify/release workflow wiring so the consent surface cannot drift silently.
